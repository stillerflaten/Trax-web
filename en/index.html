NewTrack plans in version 2.6

Follow the track. See the progress.

Trax records the track while you lay it, tells you when your dog is getting close to an item, and shows you afterwards how well your dog held the track.

$11.99, once. No subscription, no account, no ads.

How it works

From plan to result, in one app.

  1. 1

    Plan the track

    iPhone

    Draw legs and items on the map at home, and send the plan to the track layer.

    • Length as you drawFor each leg and the whole track.
    • GroupsSee if tracks cross or lie too close.
    • Share the planStraight into Trax or as GPX.
  2. 2

    Lay the track

    Tap “Item” where you drop something. Trax records the route with GPS, counts the track age and alerts you when the track is ready.

    • Metres between itemsSee the distance since the last item.
    • Track ageCountdown on the lock screen.
    • Hold to stopThe track never ends by accident.
  3. 3

    Track with your dog

    Keep your phone in your pocket. You hear it when you get close to an item, and the phone vibrates if you leave the track.

    • Voice alerts“Item nearby” before you reach it.
    • Off trackVibration when you leave the track.
    • Automatic findsRecorded when you get there.
  4. 4

    See what happened

    Play the track back, see the deviation along the route and how many items your dog found. Over time you see the progress of each dog.

    • PlaybackWatch the whole track again.
    • DeviationA graph along the whole track.
    • AssessmentRating, difficulty and a learning point.

Features

One app for all your tracking work.

Tracks, IGP tracks, blood tracks, article search and area search in the same app.

iPhone

Track plans

Plan your training day at home. Draw the tracks on the map, group them and see at once if any of them cross.

Track detail

Playback, deviation, items and conditions in one place.

IGP tracks

Levels from IGP-V to FH 3, a template on the map and steps against the requirement.

Blood tracks

Wound beds, more blood, game and flight direction.

Article and area search

Draw the field, set a time limit and see the time for each find.

The dog page

Key figures, goals, training elements and the progress of each dog.

Night mode

Red light on the screen when you train in the dark.
