How Trax handles your data

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information Trax collects, why, and what rights you have. Trax is developed by Silje Tillerflaten, who can be reached at post@trax-dogtracking.no.

In short

Everything you record in Trax is stored only on your own phone. Trax has no account, no server of its own and no ads, and the developer has no access to your data. Trax is available for both iPhone and Android. Where the two versions use different services, what applies to each is described below.

What the app stores on your phone

Location
GPS points while you record a track or a training session. Location is only used while a session is in progress, also when the screen is locked.
Training data
Tracks, routes, times, track age, weather, notes and information about your dog that you enter yourself.
Photos and camera
If you take a photo or pick your own photo for a track’s share image, the app uses the camera or only the one photo you pick. The photo is stored on your phone. If you choose to save the share image to Photos, Trax can only add photos there, not see your photos.

When data leaves your phone

Weather data
On iPhone, the app sends the location and time where the track started, and where the dog started the track, to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) to get the weather for a session. Apple uses the location only to answer the request and does not link it to you or your Apple Account. Older sessions may have weather data from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com, CC BY 4.0). On Android, the app sends the location, rounded to about 100 metres, and the time to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com, Switzerland) when you lay a track, when the dog starts, and when you tap “Get weather”. No account, names or training data are sent. The weather data that comes back is stored with the session on your phone.
Place names
When you stop a track, the app suggests a name with the place where the track started, for example “Byneset Oct 25, 8:15 AM”. To find the place name, the coordinates of the starting point are sent to the Norwegian Mapping Authority’s place name service (Kartverket) when you are in Norway, and elsewhere to Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android). On Android, the location is rounded to about 100 metres before it is sent. No names, training data or anything else that identifies you is sent, and the place name is only stored as the name of the track on your phone. Without a connection, the name is the date and time instead.
Maps
Map images are loaded from Apple Maps on iPhone and from Google Maps on Android, or from Kartverket when you choose the topographic map. The service then knows which part of the map is shown, as with any map app. Google processes such data under Google’s Privacy Policy.
Location on Android
On Android, your location comes from Google’s location services on the phone (Google Play services). If Google Location Accuracy is turned on, Google may use Wi-Fi and mobile networks to determine the location, according to the settings you have chosen with Google. Trax does not send your tracks anywhere.
Export
When you choose to export or share a session, it is sent wherever you choose, for example by email, message or to another app. This only happens when you tap export.
Backup
On iPhone, iOS may include your Trax data in your iCloud Backup if it is turned on, and Trax can put an automatic copy in iCloud Drive. This is controlled by Apple and your Apple Account, not by Trax. On Android, Trax is excluded from Google’s automatic backup. Instead, the app can put an automatic copy in a folder you choose. If that folder syncs with a cloud service, such as Google Drive, the copy is governed by that service and your own choice.
Crash reports
Anonymous crash reports from Apple, only if you have turned on sharing of analytics data on your iPhone. On Android, Google Play may send anonymous crash reports if you have turned on sharing of usage and diagnostics data with Google.

Trax does not sell data, does not use it for advertising and does not track you across other apps and websites.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub stores visitors' IP addresses in technical logs for security purposes, see GitHub's privacy statement. The website does not use cookies and does not load fonts from other services. To see how many people visit the site, I use GoatCounter, which counts page views anonymously. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not store your IP address and cannot follow you across websites. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long data is kept

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual sessions in the app, and everything is removed permanently when you delete the app.

Your rights

Since your data is stored only with you, you have full control over it yourself. You can turn off location access at any time under Settings › Trax on iPhone, or under Settings › Apps › Trax › Permissions on Android. If you have questions about privacy, contact post@trax-dogtracking.no. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).

Children

Trax is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information about them.

Changes

I update this policy when needed. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
