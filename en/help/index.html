Common questions

  • The track is inaccurate

    Check that Trax has access to your location “While Using the App” and that Precise Location is on under Settings › Trax › Location. GPS is less precise in dense forest and between tall buildings.

  • How do I delete my data?

    Everything is stored only on your phone. You can delete single sessions in the app, or delete the app to remove all data permanently.

  • Can I move my data to a new phone?

    Your data comes along when you restore the phone from an iCloud or computer backup. You can also export sessions from the app.

Privacy

Everything you record in Trax is stored only on your own phone. Trax has no account, no server of its own and no ads, and I have no access to your data.
